Our Story

More than a coffee shop

KAtambayAN was born from a simple Filipino truth — the best conversations happen over a cup of coffee. We are not just a kapehan; we are a tambayan. A place where neighbors become friends, students find a second home, and ideas brew alongside the espresso.

We serve hand-crafted coffee using locally sourced beans from Cavite, Batangas, and Benguet — supporting Filipino farmers while creating spaces of genuine belonging.

Every cup is poured with malasakit — that uniquely Filipino sense of care. Because here, you are not just a customer. You are kasama.

⚠ Why Clear Job Roles Prevent Conflict

One of the most common causes of workplace tension in small businesses is confusion over who is responsible for what. When roles are not clearly defined, tasks get duplicated, ignored, or argued over.

At KAtambayAN, every employee receives a written job description on Day 1. This ensures everyone knows exactly what is expected of them — and what is not their responsibility. No overlapping authority. No room for "hindi ko trabaho 'yan."

Clear roles build trust, reduce friction, and let every team member focus on doing their best work.

Key Position Spotlight

The Barista

The Barista is the heart and face of KAtambayAN. This role was chosen as our key position because it directly shapes every customer's experience — from first greeting to last sip.

Barista

Full-time · Reports to: Head Barista & Owner–Manager

Now Hiring

Job Summary

A Barista at KAtambayAN is responsible for preparing and serving high-quality beverages, maintaining a clean and organized workspace, and creating a warm and welcoming experience for every customer who walks through our door. This is not just a service job — it is a hosting role.

Duties & Responsibilities

  • Prepare and serve all coffee and beverage menu items following KAtambayAN's recipes and quality standards
  • Operate espresso machines, grinders, milk steamers, and related café equipment safely and correctly
  • Greet every customer warmly within 5 seconds of arrival and guide them through the menu when needed
  • Maintain cleanliness of the bar counter, equipment, and surrounding areas at all times
  • Restock supplies, ingredients, and cups during off-peak hours to ensure smooth service
  • Handle cash and POS transactions with accuracy and honesty when the cashier is unavailable
  • Collaborate with teammates to maintain fast, efficient, and friendly service — especially during rush hours
  • Report any equipment issues, stock shortages, or customer complaints promptly to the Head Barista
  • Follow all food safety, hygiene, and sanitation standards at all times
  • Participate in team meetings, trainings, and product knowledge sessions as scheduled

Required Education & Qualifications

  • Minimum Senior High School graduate (SHS Diploma required)
  • TESDA NC II in Bread and Pastry or Barista Operations is a strong advantage
  • No prior barista experience required — we provide training for qualified applicants
  • At least 6 months experience in any food service or customer-facing role is preferred
  • Basic reading and writing skills in Filipino and English

Required Skills

  • Ability to follow recipes precisely and consistently
  • Basic arithmetic for measuring ingredients and handling transactions
  • Good verbal communication — clear, friendly, and respectful
  • Ability to multitask and work efficiently under pressure
  • Physical stamina to stand for extended periods (up to 8 hours)
  • Willingness to learn and improve continuously

Required Attitude

Skills can be taught. Attitude cannot. We look for people who embody the KAtambayAN spirit:

Maaasahan — Reliable Masipag — Hardworking Magalang — Respectful May Malasakit — Genuine Care Matulungin — Helpful Maayos — Organized

Employment Classification

Types of Employment

All new hires at KAtambayAN begin as Probationary employees. Understanding your employment type protects both you and the business — it is your right to know.

Probationary
New Hire

Duration: Up to 6 months from start date.

What it means: You are being evaluated based on standards given to you on Day 1. You receive the same wages and government benefits as a regular employee.

After 6 months: If performance standards are met, you become a Regular employee. Failure to meet standards must be communicated before the probationary period ends.

Regular
Permanent

Duration: Indefinite — for as long as the employment relationship continues.

What it means: You have full security of tenure under Philippine labor law. You can only be dismissed for just or authorized causes, with proper due process.

Benefits: All statutory benefits plus eligibility for performance bonuses, salary increases, and promotion.

Contractual
Project / Seasonal

Duration: Fixed term — typically for a specific event, season, or project (e.g., holiday rush, pop-up events).

What it means: Employment ends automatically upon expiration of the contract. Still entitled to minimum wage and government-mandated benefits during the contract period.

Note: KAtambayAN follows the law on contractualization — we do not use fixed-term contracts to avoid giving regular status to long-term workers.

Recruitment & Hiring

How we hire our people

At KAtambayAN, we hire people not only for skills, but also for attitude, warmth, and malasakit. We believe coffee can be taught — kindness cannot.

Recruitment Plan

  • Online Posting: Job openings are posted on Facebook, Instagram, and local community groups.
  • School Partnerships: We welcome Senior High School and college graduates looking for first work experience.
  • Walk-In Applicants: Applicants may directly submit resumes at the café.
  • Referral System: Current employees may recommend trusted applicants.
  • Equal Opportunity: Hiring is based on qualifications, attitude, and willingness to learn — never on gender, religion, or background.
“We hire people who can grow with the team and treat others with respect.”

Hiring Process

  1. Application Submission — Resume and basic information are collected online or in person.
  2. Initial Screening — The manager reviews qualifications, availability, and attitude.
  3. Interview — Qualified applicants attend a face-to-face interview focused on communication and customer service.
  4. Trial Assessment — Applicants may perform a short café simulation or customer interaction test.
  5. Final Decision — Selected applicants receive a job offer and schedule for orientation.
  6. Orientation & Training — New hires learn workplace rules, coffee preparation, customer service, and safety procedures.

Training Plan

  • Day 1 Orientation: Introduction to company culture, policies, and workplace expectations.
  • Coffee & Beverage Training: Hands-on training for espresso preparation, recipes, and drink consistency.
  • Customer Service Training: Proper greetings, communication, and handling customer concerns.
  • Food Safety & Sanitation: Proper hygiene, cleaning procedures, and safe food handling.
  • Shadowing Period: New employees work alongside experienced staff during their first weeks.

Probationary Evaluation

All new employees undergo a probationary period of up to six (6) months. During this time, performance and attitude are carefully evaluated.

  • Attendance and punctuality
  • Ability to follow instructions and recipes
  • Customer service and communication skills
  • Teamwork and workplace attitude
  • Cleanliness and responsibility
“Regularization is earned through consistency, teamwork, and malasakit.”

💡 Simple explanation: Overtime pay means you earn MORE per hour when you work beyond 8 hours or on your rest day. Holiday pay means working on a holiday earns you double or more. These are your rights under the Philippine Labor Code — KAtambayAN will never ask you to waive them.

13th Month Pay

Under Presidential Decree No. 851, all rank-and-file employees who have worked for at least one (1) month are entitled to 13th month pay. This applies to all KAtambayAN staff.

Formula: Total Basic Salary Earned in a Year ÷ 12 = 13th Month Pay

Example: If you earned ₱16,770/month for 12 months, your 13th month pay = ₱16,770.

Deadline: Paid on or before December 24 each year. We may release it earlier as a Christmas gift to our team.

Probationary employees who leave before December are still entitled to a prorated amount based on months worked.

Mandatory Benefits

Philippine Government Benefits

From Day 1 of employment, KAtambayAN enrolls every employee in all three government-mandated benefit programs. These are your legal rights — not a privilege.

SSS

Social Security System

Social Security System

What it is: A social insurance program that provides financial assistance during sickness, maternity, disability, retirement, and death.

Who pays: Both employee and employer share the monthly contribution.

Employee share: Approximately 4.5% of monthly salary.

Employer share: Approximately 9.5% of monthly salary.

Benefits include: Sickness benefit, maternity benefit, retirement pension, disability grant, and death/funeral benefit for dependents.

Phil
Health

PhilHealth

Philippine Health Insurance Corporation

What it is: National health insurance that helps cover hospitalization, surgeries, consultations, medicines, and other medical expenses.

Who pays: Employee and employer split the contribution equally.

Monthly contribution: 5% of monthly salary — split 50/50 between you and the employer.

Benefits include: In-patient and out-patient coverage, Z-benefits for serious illness, maternity care, and case-rate packages for common procedures.

Pag-
IBIG

Pag-IBIG Fund

Home Development Mutual Fund (HDMF)

What it is: A national savings program that helps members build savings and access affordable housing and multi-purpose loans.

Who pays: Both employee and employer contribute monthly.

Monthly contribution: ₱100–₱200 employee share (based on salary bracket). Employer matches or exceeds this.

Benefits include: Housing loan, multi-purpose loan (for emergencies, education, medical), and a savings fund you can withdraw upon retirement or after 20 years.

📋 What this means for you: Every month, KAtambayAN remits your contributions and the employer's share directly to SSS, PhilHealth, and Pag-IBIG. You will receive your SSS, PhilHealth, and Pag-IBIG numbers and member IDs during your first week of work. These benefits are yours to keep even if you eventually leave the company.

Performance & Motivation

How we grow together

Good work deserves to be recognized. At KAtambayAN, we have a simple, fair, and transparent system for evaluating performance — and for rewarding those who go above and beyond.

I.

How Employees Are Evaluated

Monthly Check-In: A brief 15-minute one-on-one conversation with the Head Barista or Owner. No forms — just honest feedback from both sides.

Twice-Yearly Formal Review: A structured written evaluation every 6 months covering five areas:

  • Quality of work and product consistency
  • Punctuality and attendance record
  • Teamwork and attitude toward co-workers
  • Customer service and communication
  • Initiative and willingness to learn

You rate us too: Employees also submit anonymous feedback about management. We use this to improve how we lead.

II.

How Good Performance Is Rewarded

  • Quarterly Performance Bonus — up to ₱2,000 for employees who meet or exceed all five evaluation criteria.
  • Barista of the Month — recognition posted in-store + ₱500 cash reward. Voted by the team and owner together.
  • Annual Salary Increase — merit-based raise reviewed every December. High performers receive a higher percentage increase.
  • Promotion Pathway — Barista → Head Barista → Shift Leader → Store Supervisor. Every step has a salary increase and added responsibilities.
  • Certificate of Recognition — issued for every major milestone: 6 months, 1 year, 3 years of service.
III.

Simple Motivation Ideas

  • Free coffee and one meal per shift — you made it, you deserve to enjoy it.
  • Birthday Off with Pay — your birthday is a paid rest day, no questions asked.
  • Flexible Scheduling — for students and parents, we work around your class or family schedule whenever possible.
  • Team Outing — quarterly bonding activity. Lakad-lakad with the crew, paid by the shop.
  • Surprise Tokens — small gifts during Christmas, team milestones, or just because. Pasasalamat goes a long way.
  • Open Door Policy — anyone can speak to the owner directly about concerns, ideas, or suggestions. No hierarchy when it comes to being heard.

Workplace Culture

Our house rules

A workplace where everyone feels safe, respected, and proud to come to work every day.

House Rules

  1. Be on time. If late, message the manager 30 minutes ahead.
  2. Wear the uniform clean and pressed every shift.
  3. No personal phones at the counter during service hours.
  4. Greet every customer warmly within 5 seconds of arrival.
  5. Keep your station clean as you go — bawal ang kalat.
  6. Help your teammate before they have to ask. That is malasakit.

Attendance & Discipline

  • Tardiness: 3 instances/month triggers a written warning.
  • Absences: Inform at least 24 hours in advance.
  • Sick leave: Submit medical cert within 3 working days.
  • Discipline steps: Verbal → Written → Suspension → Termination.
  • Due process: Every employee is heard before any final decision.

Anti-Harassment Policy

KAtambayAN has zero tolerance for harassment, discrimination, or bullying — between co-workers, from customers, or anywhere in our space.

Any concern can be reported privately to the manager or owner. All reports are taken seriously, investigated fairly, and handled with full confidentiality.
