Our Story

More than a coffee shop

KAtambayAN was born from a simple Filipino truth — that the best conversations happen over a cup of coffee. We're not just a kapehan; we're a tambayan. A place where neighbors become friends, where students find their second home, where ideas brew alongside the espresso.

Located in the heart of Pampanga, we serve hand-crafted coffee with locally-sourced beans from Cavite, Batangas, and Benguet — supporting Filipino farmers while creating spaces of belonging.

Every cup we serve is poured with malasakit — that uniquely Filipino sense of genuine care. Because to us, you're not just a customer. You're kasama.

⚠ Why Clear Job Roles Prevent Conflict

One of the most common causes of workplace tension in small businesses is confusion over who is responsible for what. When roles are not clearly defined, tasks get duplicated, ignored, or argued over.

At KAtambayAN, every employee receives a written job description on Day 1. This ensures everyone knows exactly what is expected of them — and what is not their responsibility. No overlapping authority. No room for "hindi ko trabaho 'yan."

Clear roles build trust, reduce friction, and let every team member focus on doing their best work.

About the Role

As a Barista at KAtambayAN, you're the heart of the customer experience. You'll craft quality drinks, build relationships with regulars, and help create the warm, welcoming atmosphere that makes us a true tambayan.

Duties & Responsibilities

  • Prepare and serve coffee and other beverages following our recipes and standards
  • Operate espresso machines, grinders, and other café equipment safely
  • Greet and engage customers warmly — you're not just serving coffee, you're hosting
  • Maintain cleanliness of work area, counters, and dining space
  • Handle cash and POS transactions accurately
  • Restock ingredients and supplies during slow hours
  • Collaborate with the team to ensure smooth service during peak hours

Required Skills & Qualifications

  • At least Senior High School graduate (TESDA NC II for Barista is a plus)
  • Minimum 6 months experience in food service or customer-facing role preferred
  • Good communication skills in Filipino and basic English
  • Able to stand for long hours and work in a fast-paced environment
  • Detail-oriented, punctual, and reliable
  • Genuinely friendly and patient — must love working with people

Attitude We Look For

Maaasahan. Masipag. May malasakit. We believe skills can be taught, but attitude is everything. If you take pride in your work and treat every customer like family, you'll thrive here.

Workplace Culture

Our house rules

A workplace where everyone feels safe, respected, and proud to come to work. These are the rules we live by.

House Rules

  1. Be on time. If late, message the manager 30 minutes ahead.
  2. Wear the uniform clean and pressed. First impressions matter.
  3. No phones at the counter during service hours.
  4. Greet every customer warmly within 5 seconds of arrival.
  5. Keep your station clean as you go — bawal ang kalat.
  6. Help your teammate before they ask. That's malasakit.

Attendance & Discipline

  • Tardiness: 3 strikes/month triggers a written warning.
  • Absences: Inform the manager 24 hours in advance whenever possible.
  • Sick leave: Submit medical certificate within 3 days.
  • Disciplinary steps: Verbal warning → Written warning → Suspension → Termination.
  • Due process: Every employee is heard before any decision is final.

Anti-Harassment

KAtambayAN has zero tolerance for harassment, discrimination, or bullying of any kind — whether between coworkers, from customers, or anywhere in our workplace.

Any concern can be reported privately to the manager or owner. All reports are taken seriously, investigated fairly, and handled with confidentiality.

Performance & Recognition

How we grow together

We believe in fair evaluation and meaningful recognition. Here's how we measure success — and how we reward it.

I.

How We Evaluate

Monthly check-ins between you and your manager. Twice-yearly formal reviews based on customer service, teamwork, punctuality, and product knowledge. Feedback flows both ways — you get to evaluate us, too.

II.

How We Reward

Quarterly performance bonus (up to ₱2,000). "Barista of the Month" recognition with cash bonus. Annual salary review tied to performance. Promotion path to Shift Lead and Manager for those who shine.

III.

Free coffee and meals during shifts. Birthday off with pay. Flexible scheduling for students and parents. Team outings every quarter. Surprise tokens of appreciation. Because pasasalamat goes a long way.
